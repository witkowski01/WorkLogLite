Privacy policy

Effective from 5 October 2026.

In short. Worklog Lite has no server and does not collect your data. Time entries and reports travel directly between your device and your Jira. The only exception is the free iPhone, iPad and Android version: it shows Google ads, and Google receives basic technical data about the device when it does. After the “Remove ads” purchase, the Google ads module is not started. The macOS, Windows and Linux versions have no ads.

1. Developer and contact

Worklog Lite for Jira (“the app”) is made by Konrad Witkowski. Contact for all privacy matters: witkompapp@gmail.com.

The developer does not collect or store your data. The GDPR and the App Store and Google Play rules still require a privacy policy to say who publishes the app and how to contact them. For ads (section 3), the developer is responsible together with Google for the ads module in the app sending data to Google.

2. Data that stays on your device

DataWhere and for how long
Jira address, email and tokenIn the app's memory until you close it. If you check “Remember on this device”, in the system keychain until you remove them with “Remove saved sign-in details”.
Time entries, issues, reportsDownloaded from your Jira and kept only in the app's memory. They are gone when you close the app.
Interface languageOnly the language code (e.g. en) in the app's local storage.
CSV exportA file you save yourself, in a place you choose or in the app's folder.

Connections to Jira go directly from your device to the address you enter, with your permissions. The author of the app has no access to this data and does not receive it.

3. Ads in the iPhone, iPad and Android version

The free mobile version shows one bar with an ad served by Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area and Switzerland the service is provided by Google Ireland Limited, elsewhere by Google LLC. Google processes ad data as an independent controller under the Google Privacy Policy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What Google receives

  • the IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate location (city or region),
  • device and system information, such as the model, system version, language and time zone,
  • identifiers tied to the app on the device (such as the identifier for vendor on iOS or the app set ID on Android),
  • ad information: which ads were shown and whether they were tapped,
  • diagnostic data from the ads module, such as launch time and errors,
  • the address of the page describing the app, which Google uses to match ads to its topic.

What Google does not receive

  • anything from your Jira: its address, sign-in details, issues, entries or reports,
  • the device's advertising identifier. On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), and on Android it does not have the Google advertising ID permission.

Consent

In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent window appears the first time you open the app. If you agree, Google may use the device's local storage and personalise ads (legal basis: consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you don't, Google shows limited ads, without cookies or local storage and without profiling. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the “Ad privacy settings” button in the “Remove ads” window.

4. The “Remove ads” purchase

The purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. The author of the app does not receive card or payment details. The app only checks on the device whether your store account has the “Remove ads” purchase, and stores nothing about it. After the purchase the Google ads module is not started and the app does not connect to the ad network.

5. Who receives data

Only Google (ads, section 3) and Apple or Google as the store operator (purchase, section 4). The author of the app does not sell data or share it with anyone else. Google may process data outside the EEA with the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict its processing and to data portability, the right to object, and the right to withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl), or the authority in your country.

The author of the app does not store your data, so for ad data please contact Google (myaccount.google.com). If you have questions, write to witkompapp@gmail.com and I will help.

7. Children

The app is a work tool and is not directed at children. Ad requests are not marked as child-directed.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy will appear on this page together with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes in the store.
