Worklog Lite for Jira

Quick time logging and monthly reports from Jira, without installing anything in Jira. The app connects to Jira with your own permissions, just like your browser does.

Coming soon to the App Store Coming soon to Google Play macOS · Windows · Linux

What it does

Log time

Find an issue by key or part of its name, then set the date, start time and duration (1h 30m, 90m, 1.5). The next entry starts where the previous one ended.

Week view

Hour bars for every day, the day's entries with a total, editing and deleting. Entries added in Jira in the browser show up on their own.

Monthly report

A list of entries or an issues × days timesheet with totals. Excel-compatible CSV export and copying the table into a spreadsheet.

New issues

If the issue doesn't exist yet, create it from the search, with the fields the project requires, and log time on it right away.

Jira Cloud and Data Center

Cloud with an API token, or Server / Data Center with a Personal Access Token (Jira 8.14+).

Four languages

English, Polish, German and Italian, with matching date and number formats.

Privacy at a glance

We have no intermediary server: Jira data travels directly between your device and your Jira. Working copies of time entries, issues and reports stay in memory while the app runs; data saved in Jira remains in Jira. Sign-in details are saved on your device only when you ask: in the system keychain, or on Android in a private file encrypted with an Android Keystore key. Signing out does not remove saved details — use the separate removal button for that.

Your chosen language is saved locally. The light or dark theme follows your system settings, with no separate saved preference. Exported CSV files and data copied to the clipboard may remain after the app closes.

The iPhone, iPad and Android version shows a slim Google ad bar; after two failed ad loads, it may show its own ad-free offer. A one-time “Remove ads” purchase removes both bars and can be restored on devices using the same account in the same store. Nothing from your Jira is sent to Google, but the ads and store modules process their own data. Details are in the privacy policy.

Help

How do I sign in to Jira?

Jira Cloud: create an API token at id.atlassian.com and enter your Jira address, Atlassian account email and the token in the app. Jira Server / Data Center: in Jira, open your profile, choose Personal Access Tokens and create a token.

I bought “Remove ads”, but I see ads on my new phone

Sign in to the store with the same account (Apple ID or Google account) you used for the purchase. Then tap “Remove ads” in the app, or “Go ad-free” on the sign-in screen, and choose “Restore purchase”.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app uses Google’s consent form when required. When Google makes changes to your choices available, the “Remove ads” window shows an “Ad privacy settings” button, where you can change or withdraw consent.
